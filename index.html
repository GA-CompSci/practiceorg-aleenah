About Active Minds

Active Minds at Gilmour Academy is a student-led club dedicated to breaking the stigma around mental health. We promote open conversations, support student well-being, and strive to create a safe, inclusive environment where everyone feels heard and valued.

Who?

Anyone! We welcome all grade levels and accept new memebers all year long!

Topics

We talk about mental health, have speakers, organize projects and much more!

Projects
